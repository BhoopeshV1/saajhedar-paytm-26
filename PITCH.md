# Three-minute pitch

Team Finance Tours. Present from a laptop in the phone frame, speaker on, demo reset just before.

## Hook

> "Before Selvam had a shop, he had five lakh rupees and one question: what do I open, and where?"

## Script

**0:00 Hook.** Splash on screen. Say the line above, then tap Proceed.

**0:08 Before the shop.** "Thinking of opening one?" Pick Tambaram, bakery, 5 lakh. "Saajhedar tells him the truth: this spot is already crowded. It shows what the area is short of instead." Open the map. "Same spot, his competitors only, and what he would start with: a QR, a Soundbox, and a loan sized to his gap."

**0:35 The turn.** "He opened a grocery. Like most kiranas he has no billing app, just a paper pad, and he calls out every item as he writes it."

**0:42 The pad becomes a bill book.** Tap the mic and say: one kilo sugar, two milk packets, cash. "That is a bill. The stock just dropped. He never touched the phone."

**1:02 It works before he does.** Open Stock. "Karthigai Deepam is nine days away and lamp oil runs out tomorrow. And at the top of every screen are his rules, in his own words."

**1:15 It picks up the phone.** Tap "Call suppliers and order". Let the call play for about ten seconds, then "Skip to the result". "Saajhedar rang Sri Balaji and ordered. The supplier was short on camphor, so it took what he had. In this prototype the supplier's side is simulated." Tap "Next call", then "Done". The PIN pad appears. "KPN's order is above his limit of 8,000 rupees, so it stops and asks him."

**1:50 Stock becomes Pay.** Tap "See the invoice in Pay". "The invoice is already here, and it will not be paid until the delivery is counted." Photograph the paper bill. "Thirty kilos of jaggery billed, twenty-five delivered. It offers to pay the right amount."

**2:15 He stays in charge.** Say: "Pay KPN Traders up to fifteen thousand without asking." The PIN pad appears. "Saajhedar proposes. Money moves only inside rules he speaks and signs."

**2:30 Why Paytm.** "Paytm has the QR, the Soundbox, the settlements and the lending partners. Saajhedar is the reason a person on the fence becomes a merchant, and the reason that merchant opens the app every morning."

**2:50 Close.** "Saajhedar. Your shop's partner, from the first question to the daily shutter. We are Finance Tours."

## If you run long

1. Drop 2:15, the spoken rule. The KPN PIN stop at 1:15 already shows it.
2. Then drop the bill photo at 1:50 and keep only the invoice landing in Pay.

## Before you go on

- Reset the demo, so the suppliers have not been called yet.
- Play one supplier call beforehand so its voices are already saved and start at once.
- Say the numbers the screen shows on the day.
- Kept for questions: the customer's own phone, udhaar reminders, the billing-app export, Tamil and Hindi.
