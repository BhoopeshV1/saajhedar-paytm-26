# Three-minute pitch

Team Finance Tours. Present from a laptop in the phone frame, speaker on, demo reset just before.

The story has three acts, and the first is the hero: **start** a shop knowing the area, **run** it by voice, **grow** it with the same knowledge.

## Hook

> "Before Selvam had a shop, he had five lakh rupees and one question: what do I open, and where?"

## Script

### Act 1. Start with knowledge (the hero, about a minute)

**0:00 Hook.** Splash on screen. Say the line above, then tap Proceed.

**0:08 Three questions.** Tap "Thinking of opening one?". East Tambaram, Grocery and 5 lakh are already filled in, so it is three taps: Next, Confirm, then "Show me the area". "Where, what, and how much. Nothing else."

**0:22 The scan.** Tap "Show me the area" and let it play. Talk over it: "This is real data. It finds his street, and every pin that drops is a grocery that is already open there." Do not skip it; it is eight seconds and it is the picture people remember.

**0:32 The honest answer.** The result opens. "Fourteen groceries here. Areas this busy usually have eight. Saajhedar tells him the truth: already crowded." Scroll once. "It compared this spot with forty similar areas, and it shows what this one is short of instead: an optician, a stationery shop, each with what it costs to open."

**0:50 Knowledge, then a start.** Point at "Ask Saajhedar AI what to open". "One tap and the AI reads these numbers and gives him two or three picks, each with a reason and a risk. Below it, what he would start with: a QR, a Soundbox, and a loan sized to his gap."

### Act 2. Run it by voice (about a minute)

**1:02 The turn.** Tap "See it on the map" at the bottom of the result; that opens the shop app on its Area view. Then tap Sell. "He opened his shop. Like most kiranas he has no billing app, just a paper pad, and he calls out every item as he writes it."

**1:08 The pad becomes a bill book.** Tap the mic and say: one kilo sugar, two milk packets, cash. "That is a bill. The stock just dropped. He never touched the phone."

**1:25 It picks up the phone.** Open Stock. "Karthigai Deepam is nine days away and lamp oil runs out tomorrow." Tap "Call suppliers and order". Let the call play for about eight seconds, then "Skip to the result". "Saajhedar rang the supplier and ordered. In this prototype the supplier's side is simulated." Tap "Next call", then "Done".

**1:48 He stays in charge.** Point at the yellow notice. "KPN's order is above his limit of 8,000 rupees, so it did not go. It waits for him." Tap Approve to show the PIN pad, then "Not now". "Saajhedar proposes. Money moves only inside rules he speaks and signs."

**2:00 Stock becomes Pay.** Tap "See the invoice in Pay". "The invoice is already here, and it will not be paid until the delivery is counted."

### Act 3. Grow with the same knowledge (about half a minute)

**2:08 Three ways to grow.** Go Home and tap "Grow my shop". "A year on, the shop works and Selvam wants more. The data that helped him start now answers three new questions." Tap through the three tabs as you say them.

- "**A second branch.** The same shop in another area: busy places nearby with fewer groceries than usual." Tap the first row; the map opens on that area with its groceries marked.
- "**A bigger shop.** What is sold thinly around his own shop, so he knows which shelf to add."
- "**A different shop.** What his neighbourhood is short of, with what each costs to open."

"Every one of those is the same question he asked on day one, asked again with a merchant's track record behind it."

### Close

**2:35 Why Paytm.** "Paytm has the QR, the Soundbox, the settlements and the lending partners. Saajhedar is the reason a person on the fence becomes a merchant, the reason that merchant opens the app every morning, and the reason his second shop is a Paytm shop too."

**2:52 Close.** "Saajhedar. Your shop's partner, from the first question to the next branch. We are Finance Tours."

## If you run long

1. Drop 2:00, the invoice in Pay.
2. Then shorten the supplier call: skip to the result at once.
3. Never cut the scan or the three ways to grow. They are the pitch.

## If something fails on stage

- **The mic does not hear you.** The bars inside the mic button stay flat. Tap the keyboard button at the top right, type "one kilo sugar, two milk packets, cash", and carry on.
- **The scan stalls.** Tap Skip; the result still opens.

## Before you go on

- Reset the demo, so the suppliers have not been called yet.
- Play one supplier call beforehand so its voices are already saved and start at once.
- Run Act 1 once beforehand and say the numbers the screen shows on the day; the ones above are from East Tambaram on 15 November.
- Test the mic in the room: tap it and check the bars move when you speak.
- Kept for questions: photographing a paper bill (billed against delivered), speaking a new rule, the customer's own phone, udhaar reminders, the billing-app export, Tamil and Hindi.

## What to say if asked

- **Is the growth part built?** Yes: "Grow my shop" on Home answers all three from the same area data, and one tap asks the AI to compare them. It does not yet use the shop's own sales to size a second branch.
- **Is the analysis AI?** The counts and the comparison with similar areas are plain code on open map data. The AI adds the judgment: which option, why, and what to watch out for.
- **What does "similar areas" mean?** Areas with about as many businesses listed nearby. It does not yet account for income, rents or footfall.
- **How complete is the data?** Open map listings miss small shops with no online presence, so counts are a guide, not a census.
